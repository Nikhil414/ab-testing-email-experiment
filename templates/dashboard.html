How to read this fairly
  • Extra purchases: the email group's purchase rate minus the no-email rate, scaled to 1,000 assigned customers. Percentage-point lift and relative percentage lift are different measures.
  • Uncertainty: each interval is 97.5%. The two planned purchase comparisons use Bonferroni correction. The revenue intervals form a separate secondary analysis; joint coverage across all four outcomes is not claimed. Normal/t approximations are used; revenue is skewed.
  • Data checks: $allocation_note There are $repeated_rows repeated complete records, retained because original customer IDs are unavailable. Identity, independence, and cross-group contamination cannot be independently verified.
  • Scope: a randomized historical experiment from 2008. No direct statistical comparison between the two email campaigns was performed. The visual conclusions use calculated evidence and do not rely on an AI approval.
